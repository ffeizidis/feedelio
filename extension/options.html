Feedelio companion

A small bridge between your browser and your reading space.

Reading List URLs, titles and read states are sent only to the server above. Read changes sync in both directions, and removing a page from the Reading List removes it from Feedelio.

Your playlists, Watch Later included, are read with your YouTube sign-in in this browser. Only playlist and video ids and titles are sent to the server above; your sign-in never leaves the browser. Feedelio keeps each video's details, thumbnail and transcript, even if YouTube later removes the video.
